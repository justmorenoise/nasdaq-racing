# Audio credits

The engine is synthesised in `src/audio/AudioEngine.ts`, voiced with material
derived from real recordings by `_risorse/audio_src/make_engine.py`:

- `engine.json` — harmonic spectrum (the oscillator's PeriodicWave) of
  [Williams-Cosworth FW32 (2010).ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Williams-Cosworth_FW32_(2010).ogg),
  Goodwood Festival of Speed 2010, by Ed Pond (Edvvc), **CC BY-SA 3.0**.
- `engine_noise.wav` — non-harmonic residue (combustion, exhaust and mechanical
  noise) of [Ferrari F60 (2009).ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_F60_(2009).ogg),
  Goodwood Festival of Speed 2010, by Ed Pond (Edvvc), **CC BY-SA 3.0**.

Both are derived works distributed under the same licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

- `tyres.wav` — [Car tire squeal skid loop](https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop)
  by Tom Haigh (audible-edge), **CC BY 3.0**, resampled to 44.1 kHz mono.

Gear-change ticks, overrun crackles and the team-radio blip are synthesised.
